Setup Time

Overview

What is Rally?

Rally is your all-in-one platform for sourcing, scheduling, tracking, and incentivizing participant research. Stop managing spreadsheets and manual follow-ups. Automate the logistics so you can focus on insights.

Without Rally
  • Contacts are scattered across spreadsheets
  • Scheduling conflicts and manual booking back-and-forth
  • Follow up reminders slip through the cracks
  • No real-time visibility into participant status or no-show rates
  • Multiple point solutions needed to achieve your research goals
With Rally
  • All contacts, responses, and bookings in one centralized dashboard
  • Customers self-schedule via a single booking link with no back-and-forth
  • Automated reminders and follow-ups for no-shows
  • Real-time tracking of confirmations and no-show rates
  • Source, schedule, and manage participants from a single dashboard
  • Send outreach directly through approved Procore templates and workflows
  • Schedule calls via Gmail, Google Calendar, and Zoom integrations
  • Invite teammates to join calls as observers
  • Reuse participant lists by duplicating past studies
Getting Started

Your Role and Permissions

There are two ways to work in Rally. Your role determines your workflow in the platform and your permissions within it.

Private
Rally users who want to manage outreach independently.
Permissions
Private List
Your own customer lists that you upload into Rally to conduct calls and research with
Rally Opted-in Database
You will not have access to the Rally Opted-in Database
List Visibility
These lists can only be viewed, accessed, and edited by the list owner
Best for Rally users who want to keep their outreach and customer lists private.
Shared
Rally users who want access to a broader shared pool.
Permissions
Private List
No access to private lists
Shared List
A customer pool shared across Rally users
Research Program
Opt-in research participants (incentivized)
Best for Rally users who want access to a broader shared pool of participants.
Note: You can update your role later in your account settings to be able to conduct both Private + Shared research in Rally.

Getting Started: 4-Step Onboarding

Complete these steps in order. Product Operations will guide you through the process.

1
Request Rally Access

Submit an IT ticket to request your Rally login. If unsure which request type to use, ask in #rally on Slack.

Outcome

You'll receive a Rally invite via email once access is approved.

2
Prepare and Submit Your Customer List

Prepare your customer list using the Rally Import List template. The template link will prompt you to create your own copy automatically.

What to do
  1. Click the template link and create your own copy.
  2. Add your customers and include the required details, including Account ID.
  3. Choose your list type:
    • Private List: Only you will use it.
    • Shared List: Others with shared access can use it.

    Note: If you need both, prepare two separate lists: one private and one shared.

  4. Post in #rally when your list is ready to upload.
  5. Product Operations will follow up in Slack with next steps.
3
Attend Your Setup Session

Once your access is approved and list is uploaded, Product Operations will schedule a setup session with you. During this session, you'll connect your tools and verify your participant list is loaded correctly.

Connect Your Tools (One-Time Setup)
Onboarding Step

Connect your Gmail, Google Calendar, and Zoom during onboarding. Once done, they'll stay active for future use.

What to do
  • Hover over your workspace name in the top left corner and click Settings
  • Select User > Connected Accounts
  • Connect Google (Gmail + Calendar) and Zoom

Part 2

Rally Outreach and Scheduling

Now that you've completed Part 1, you're set up in Rally and connected to Zoom and Google Calendar. The steps below are the repeating workflow you'll run every time you launch a new round of customer calls. Both Private (your own customer list) and Shared (verified pool / research program) paths are covered. Pick the one that matches the IT ticket you submitted.

1
Duplicate a Previous Study
Reuse your customer list by duplicating your most recent study. It's faster than starting from scratch.
New to Rally?

If you haven't run a study before, your Product Operations contact will set up your first one during your onboarding session. Come back to this step once your first study exists.

Rather than starting from scratch, duplicate a past study to carry your customer list forward.

What to do
  • In the left sidebar, click Studies
  • Check the box next to the study you want to duplicate
  • Click Duplicate in the toolbar
  • Make sure "Include participants" is checked, then click Duplicate study
Step 1 · Part 1: Click Studies, check the box, click Duplicate
Step 1 · Part 2: Check Include participants, then click Duplicate study
Important

Always check "Include participants" when duplicating. If you miss it, your customer list won't transfer. You'll need to re-import manually.

2
Update Your Study
Review and edit your study details before launching.

Update the study name, scope, and session details as needed. If you need to add more customers to your list, send the updated list to Sophia Nitti or Emanuel Ruiz-Lebrón before you launch.

What to do
  • Update study details as needed (name, session length, dates)
  • Review and update messages for this round of outreach
  • To add more customers: send them to Sophia Nitti or Emanuel Ruiz-Lebrón and they will add them to your study
3
Launch and Schedule Your Calls
Send outreach to your customer list and schedule calls through Rally.

Once everything is ready, send your outreach and let Rally handle the scheduling. Customers will receive an invitation and can book directly from the link.

What to do
  • Review the study one final time: confirm details, messages, and participant list
  • Click Send Outreach to launch
  • Monitor responses and bookings in Rally as they come in
