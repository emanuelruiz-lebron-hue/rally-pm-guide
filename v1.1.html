Overview

What is Rally?

No more spreadsheets, email threads, or manual follow-up. Rally is where you manage the full customer call process: outreach, scheduling, and tracking, all from one place.

Spend less time managing logistics. More time having the conversations that matter.

What You Can Do in Rally

In Rally, you can:

Create and manage your calls from Rally's dashboard
Send outreach directly through your approved Procore workflow
Schedule calls using your Gmail, Calendar, and Zoom integrations
Track responses, confirmations, and call status in one view
Invite observers to join calls via observation rooms
Duplicate a past study to reuse your customer list. No re-importing needed.
Getting Started

Choose Your Path

Before you start, pick the path that fits how you work. Your choice determines which Rally team you join and how your customer list is organized.

PM Private List
Private
Your list is yours alone. Only you can see and access it.
PM Shared List
Shared
Your list is visible to other PMs on your team. You can all work from it.
Both
Private + Shared
You have customers in both buckets. Some stay private; others are shared across PMs.

Getting Started in Rally

Complete these steps before your first login. Product Operations will walk you through setup. You don't do this alone.

1
Request Access to Rally
Submit an IT ticket to request your Rally login.

Access is managed through IT. Once your ticket is approved, you'll receive your login credentials and can move to the next step.

What to do
  • Complete the IT ticket to request access to Rally
  • If you are unsure of the ticket type, ask Sophia or Emanuel for the correct form
2
Send Your Customer List
Email your customer list to Sophia and Emanuel so they can import it into Rally.

Include your customers' details and note which path applies to you: Private, Shared, or Both. Your list will be ready before your setup session.

This can be
  • A Private List: only you will use it
  • A Shared List: can be used across PMs
What to do
  • Use the PM – Rally Import List template to prepare your list
  • Send to Sophia and Emanuel and specify whether it is a Private or Shared list
3
Schedule Your Setup Session
Book time with Sophia or Emanuel to configure your Rally workspace.

You'll go through Rally together, connect your tools, and confirm your list is loaded. When this session is done, you're live.

What to do
  • Schedule time with Sophia or Emanuel for a quick setup and Rally walkthrough
  • Bring your list type decision (Private, Shared, or Both) and your customer list to the session
Setup complete? You're ready to run your first calls.
Run Your Calls

Run Your Calls

Follow these steps every time you run customer calls in Rally. Setup only happens once. This is your repeating workflow.

1
Open Your Rally Workspace
Go to the Rally team that matches your path: Private or Shared.

Rally is organized by team. Open the one that matches your path. You may have access to both.

What to do
  • Go to Rally and sign in
  • Open PM Private List if you are working with contacts only you can access
  • Open PM Shared List if you are working with contacts shared across PMs
2
Connect Your Tools
First time only: connect Gmail, Google Calendar, and Zoom.

The first time you log in, connect your Gmail, Google Calendar, and Zoom accounts. This makes scheduling easier and faster. You only need to do this once.

What to do
  • In Rally, hover over Procore Technologies in the top left corner
  • Click Settings
  • Under User, select Connected Accounts
  • Connect Google (Gmail + Calendar) and Zoom
1 — Hover over your workspace name, click Settings
2 — User > Connected Accounts > connect Google and Zoom
First Time Only

One-time setup. After your first login, your integrations stay active. No action needed on future visits.

3
Duplicate a Previous Study
Reuse your customer list by duplicating your most recent study. It's faster than starting from scratch.

Rather than starting from scratch, duplicate a past study to carry your customer list forward.

What to do
  • In the left sidebar, click Studies
  • Check the box next to the study you want to duplicate
  • Click Duplicate in the toolbar
  • Make sure "Include participants" is checked, then click Duplicate study
1 — Click Studies, check the box, click Duplicate
2 — Check Include participants, then click Duplicate study
Important

Always check "Include participants" when duplicating. If you miss it, your customer list won't transfer. You'll need to re-import manually.

4
Update Your Study
Review and edit your study details before launching.

Update the study name, scope, and session details as needed. If you need to add more customers to your list, send the updated list to Sophia or Emanuel before you launch.

What to do
  • Update study details as needed (name, session length, dates)
  • Review and update messages for this round of outreach
  • To add more customers: send them to Sophia or Emanuel and they will add them to your study
5
Launch and Schedule Your Calls
Send outreach to your customer list and schedule calls through Rally.

Once everything is ready, send your outreach and let Rally handle the scheduling. Customers will receive an invitation and can book directly from the link.

What to do
  • Review the study one final time: confirm details, messages, and participant list
  • Click Send Outreach to launch
  • Monitor responses and bookings in Rally as they come in
