Part 2

Rally Outreach & Scheduling

After onboarding, use Rally to manage outreach, scheduling, and follow-up for your customer calls. This is the workflow you'll use each time you start a new round.

New to Rally?

Your first study is created during onboarding. After that, you can reuse and update it for future outreach.

1
Duplicate a Previous Study
Reuse your existing study and customer list to start a new round of outreach more quickly.

If you have not run a study before, your first study is created during onboarding. Come back to this step once that first study exists.

What to do
  1. In the left sidebar, click Studies
  2. Check the box next to the study you want to duplicate
  3. Click Duplicate in the toolbar
  4. Make sure "Include participants" is checked
  5. Click Duplicate study
Step 1 · Part 1: Click Studies, check the box, click Duplicate
Step 1 · Part 2: Check Include participants, then click Duplicate study
Important

Always check "Include participants" when duplicating. If you miss it, your customer list won't transfer. You'll need to re-import manually.

2
Update Your Study
Review and edit your study details before launching.

Update the study name, scope, and session details as needed. If you need to add more customers, let Product Operations know in #rally before you launch.

What to do
  • Update study details as needed (name, session length, dates)
  • Review and update messages for this round of outreach
  • To add more customers: post in #rally on Slack and Product Operations will add them to your study
3
Launch and Schedule Your Calls
Send outreach to your customer list and schedule calls through Rally.

Once everything is ready, send your outreach and let Rally handle the scheduling. Customers will receive your invitation and can book directly from the scheduling link.

What to do
  • Review the study one final time: confirm details, messages, and participant list
  • Click Send Outreach to launch
  • Monitor responses and bookings in Rally as they come in
