Overview

What is Rally?

Rally helps you manage outreach, scheduling, and tracking for customer calls in one place.

Without Rally
Spreadsheets to track contacts and outreach status
Manual emails to recruit and schedule calls
Follow up manually with no-shows and non-responders
Re-import your customer list each research cycle
With Rally
Contacts, responses, and bookings tracked automatically
Customers self-schedule via a booking link you send once
Automated reminders handle follow-up for you
Duplicate your last study to reuse your customer list in one click

What You Can Do with Rally

  • Create and manage your calls from Rally's dashboard
  • Send outreach directly through your approved Procore workflow
  • Schedule calls using your Gmail, Calendar, and Zoom integrations
  • Invite observers to join calls via observer rooms
Getting Started

Your Role in Rally

Before you start, choose the role that matches how you work. Your selection determines which Rally workspace you join and how your customer list is managed.

PM Private
Private
What this role is

You work only from your own customer list. Other PMs cannot access it.

Can do
  • Contact and schedule from your private list
  • Keep your customer list private
Can't do
  • Use the shared PM pool
  • Access Procore Research Program participants
PM Shared
Shared
What this role is

You work from the shared PM pool and can share your customer list with other PMs.

Can do
  • Contact and schedule from the shared PM pool
  • Share your customer list with other PMs
  • Work from a broader customer pool
Can't do
  • Keep a separate private-only list in this path
Private + Shared
Private + Shared
What this role is

You can work from your private list and the shared PM pool.

Can do
  • Contact and schedule from your private list
  • Use the shared PM pool
  • Keep private outreach separate from shared outreach
Can't do
  • Treat shared contacts as private

How You Access Customers

Rally gives you access to customers through three sources. Which ones are available to you depends on your role.

Private List
Private only
What it is

Your own imported customer list. Only you can view and contact these customers in Rally.

Who can use it

PMs with the PM Private role and PMs with the Private + Shared role when working from their private list.

Incentives

No incentives

Shared List
Shared + Private + Shared
What it is

A customer list shared across PMs. Customers in this list can be viewed and contacted by PMs with access to the shared workspace.

Who can use it

PMs with the PM Shared role and PMs with the Private + Shared role.

Incentives

No incentives

Procore Research Program
Shared + Private + Shared
What it is

A pool of 3,500+ customers who opted in to participate in research and can be incentivized.

Who can use it

PMs with the PM Shared role and PMs with the Private + Shared role.

Incentives

Incentivized according to the incentive guidelines

Getting Started in Rally

Complete these one-time onboarding steps before you begin using Rally. Product Operations will guide you through setup.

1
Request Access to Rally
Submit an IT ticket to request your Rally login.

After you submit the IT ticket and access is approved, you'll receive a Rally invite by email.

What to do
  • Submit the IT ticket to request Rally access
  • If you are not sure which request to use, ask in #rally on Slack
2
Send Your Customer List
Send your customer list before onboarding so Product Operations can import it into Rally.

Prepare your list and tell us whether it should be set up as a Private List or a Shared List.

List types
  • Private List: only you will use it
  • Shared List: can be used by other PMs
  • Private + Shared: send two separate lists, one Shared List and one Private List for your use only
What to do
  • Use the PM – Rally Import List template to prepare your list
  • When your list is ready, post in the #rally Slack channel to let Product Operations know it is ready for upload, and they will follow up with you to get the list(s)
  • Specify whether your list should be imported as Private or Shared
  • If you need both, send two separate lists: one Shared List and one Private List
3
Attend Your Setup Session
Product Operations will schedule time with you to complete your Rally setup.

Once your access is ready and your list is uploaded, Product Operations will schedule time on your calendar to help you get set up in Rally and prepare to run your calls.

What to do
  • Accept the calendar invite from Product Operations
  • Join the session ready to connect your tools
  • Confirm your list is loaded correctly in Rally
4
Connect Your Tools
Connect Gmail, Google Calendar, and Zoom during onboarding.
Onboarding Step

This is a one-time onboarding step. Once your accounts are connected, they stay active for future use.

What to do
  • In Rally, hover over your workspace name in the top left corner
  • Click Settings
  • Under User, select Connected Accounts
  • Connect Google (Gmail + Calendar) and Zoom
Step 4 · Part 1: Hover over your workspace name, click Settings
Step 4 · Part 2: User > Connected Accounts > connect Google and Zoom
Onboarding Step

This is a one-time setup step completed during onboarding. Once your accounts are connected, no action is needed on future visits.

Setup Complete

You're ready to start using Rally.

Your setup is complete. Use Rally to schedule and run customer calls.
