Overview

What is Rally?

Rally is a platform that helps you source customers, schedule calls, track outreach, and manage follow-up from one place, so you can spend more time on customer insights.

Centralized customer insights

Keep contacts, responses, bookings, and participant status together in one Rally dashboard.

Simpler scheduling

Let customers book from one link while Rally handles reminders, calendar sync, and follow-up.

Reusable outreach workflows

Start from approved templates, reuse past work, and invite teammates to observe customer calls.

Getting Started

Your Role and Permissions

There are two ways to work in Rally. Choose the path that matches how you want to source customers for your calls.

Both paths help you centralize outreach, scheduling, reminders, and tracking. The main difference is whether you bring your own customer list or use customers already available in Rally.

Private
For Rally users who already have a specific customer list and want to manage outreach in their own Rally workspace.
Why use this path
Bring your customer list into Rally so the calls, reminders, scheduling, and response tracking happen in one place.
Permissions
Customer Source
Your own customer lists uploaded into Rally for customer calls and insights
User-Owned Customers
Best when you want to stay focused on customers you already know or have already identified
List Visibility
Lists can only be viewed, accessed, and edited by the list owner
Shared
For Rally users who want to contribute customer lists to a shared Rally pool and use customer lists shared by other Rally users.
Why use this path
Build a stronger shared customer database. Adding your customer lists improves visibility into outreach, shows which initiatives customers have participated in, and helps teams coordinate better.
Permissions
Customer Source
Bring customer lists into Rally so they can be shared across Rally users
Shared List
Access customers contributed by other Rally users and add relevant participants to your own studies
Customer Visibility
See more context about customer participation across team initiatives
Note: You can update your role later in your account settings if you need to work with both Private and Shared customer sources.

Onboarding

First-Time Rally Setup

Complete this once before launching your first study. Product Operations will guide you through the process.

1
Request Rally Access

Submit an IT ticket to request your Rally login. Once approved, you'll receive your Rally invite by email. If unsure which request type to use, ask in #rally on Slack.

2
Prepare Your Customer List, If Needed

If you are using your own customer list, prepare it with the Rally Import List template. If you are using the Shared pool, you can skip this step and add participants from Rally User Shared List after your study is published.

If you need to upload a list
  1. Click the template link and create your own copy.
  2. Add your customers and include the required details, including Account ID.
  3. Name your list using this format: Your Name - Private or Your Name - Shared.
  4. Post in #rally when your list is ready to upload.
  5. Product Operations will follow up in Slack with next steps.
3
Attend Your Setup Session

Once your access is approved and your participant source is ready, Product Operations will schedule a setup session with you. During this session, we'll verify that your account is set up and your participant source is ready for your first study.

First Login
4
Complete Your Profile and Connect Your Tools

Before creating your first study, finish your Rally profile, connect your tools, and set your interview availability. Rally uses these settings to send outreach and schedule sessions correctly.

Open video in a new tab

What to do
  • In the top left corner, hover over Procore Technologies and select Settings
  • In My Profile, add your preferred name and profile photo
  • Go to Connected Accounts and connect Google so Rally can send emails and sync your calendar
  • Connect Zoom if you plan to run live calls
  • Go to Availability, set your timezone, and add the times you are available for interviews
  • Choose how Rally should handle calendar conflicts
  • Click Save

When to use this workflow

Use this workflow when you are bringing your own customer list into Rally and want to manage outreach in your own workspace.

1
Set up your study for the first time
Rally lets you reuse your customer list by duplicating a previous study, so you don't have to start from scratch each round.

Rather than starting from scratch, duplicate a past study to carry your customer list forward.

What to do
  • In the left sidebar, click Studies
  • Check the box next to the study you want to duplicate
  • Click Duplicate in the toolbar
  • When the duplicate modal opens, pause before clicking Duplicate study
Click Studies, check the box, then click Duplicate
!
Critical: keep your participants when you duplicate
This is the one duplicate-setting that prevents a full manual re-import.

When the duplicate modal opens, always check "Include participants" before clicking Duplicate study. If you skip this, your customer list will not transfer and you'll have to re-import the entire list manually.

Check Include participants before clicking Duplicate study
2
Update Your Study
Review and edit your study details before launching.

Update the study name, scope, and session details as needed. If you need to add more customers to your list, send the updated list to Sophia Nitti or Emanuel Ruiz-Lebrón before you launch.

What to do
  • Update study details as needed (name, session length, dates)
  • Review and update messages for this round of outreach
  • To add more customers: send them to Sophia Nitti or Emanuel Ruiz-Lebrón and they will add them to your study
i
Templates are already built for you

Rally ships with prebuilt outreach and reminder templates. You don't need to write messages from scratch. Open the Messages tab inside your study, edit the prefilled copy to match this round's scope, and save.

3
Launch and Schedule Your Calls
Send outreach to your customer list and schedule calls through Rally.

Once everything is ready, send your outreach and let Rally handle the scheduling. Customers will receive an invitation and can book directly from the link.

What to do
  • Review the study one final time: confirm details, messages, and participant list
  • Click Send Outreach to launch
  • Monitor responses and bookings in Rally as they come in

Shared Workflow

Open video in a new tab

When to use this workflow

Use this workflow when you want to contact customers from the Shared team. Create the study first, then add and contact customers after publishing.

1
Start in Shared
Create the study from the Shared team workspace.

Start from the Shared team so the study uses the right workspace and customer list.

What to do
  • Confirm you are in the Shared team
  • Click Studies
  • Click + New Study
  • Choose the template that matches your study. For PM customer calls, use Customer Call
i
Templates are already built for you

Use Customer Call for PM customer calls.

2
Set Up the Study
Add the customer-facing details and decide whether you need a screener.

Add the few details customers will see before they schedule.

What to do
  • Add an External study name. This is the name customers will see
  • Review the participation limit. PM studies default to 20 calls, but you can adjust it if needed
  • Optional: Add a screener only if you need to qualify customers or collect context before the call
i
Keep it simple

If you do not need to qualify customers or collect information before the call, skip the Screener section.

3
Confirm Scheduling
Review the invite, interviewers, availability, and observer room.

Confirm the calendar invite and booking times before customers see them.

What to do
  • Review the calendar invite, meeting length, and Zoom location
  • Confirm who will run the interviews and add any teammates, co-interviewers, or guests
  • Review available session times and adjust them if needed
  • Optional: Leave Observer Rooms on if teammates or stakeholders need to observe, or turn them off if they are not needed
!
Watch your availability

If customer-call time is blocked on your Google Calendar, Rally may hide it. Manually add or override those times so customers can book them.

4
Review Messages
Make sure the outreach and automated messages fit your study.

Check the customer-facing emails before publishing.

What to do
  • Review the scheduler, confirmation, reminder, cancellation, and reschedule messages
  • Update placeholders and edit the message copy as needed so it matches your study
  • Use the preview to confirm what customers will see
5
Publish the Study
Use Rally's final review to catch anything missing before the study goes live.

Use Rally's Review page as your final check.

What to do
  • Skip the Share section unless you need it
  • Review the final setup page
  • Fix anything Rally flags as missing or needing attention
  • When there are no issues, click Publish Study
!
Before you publish

Wait until Rally shows no setup issues. After publishing, the study is live, but customers have not been added yet.

6
Add and Contact Customers
Add customers from the Shared List and send the right outreach email.

Add customers from the Shared List, then send the right message.

What to do
  • Click Add participants, then choose Find people in Rally
  • Select Shared List
  • Choose specific customers, apply filters, or select a random sample
  • Click Compose email and choose the right message
  • Review the email, then send it to the selected customers
i
Which email should I send?

Screener = ask questions before scheduling
Scheduler = let customers book time
Custom email = send a one-off message
